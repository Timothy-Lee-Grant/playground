## CLI reading
- VERIFIED (f): table 29 on our chip. An unread forced measurement leaves drdy_press|drdy_temp (0x60) set (+ cmd_rdy 0x10);
  one burst read of DATA clears both. Basis of R3 confirmed.
- VERIFIED (g0): R2 happens on real hardware. With the pre-fix 6 ms wait, 3/30 forced writes after an x32 -> x1 change in
  normal mode were lost. Fewer than a worst-case estimate (~1/3 of tries have an x32 conversion running at the switch-back),
  so the chip may not always finish the old-settings conversion; why: unverified.
- VERIFIED (g): with the fix, 45/45 forced measurements after the same sequence; slowest 85 ms = wait for x32 pressure
  (69 ms + 10% + 1 ms ≈ 76 ms) + the x1/x1 forced read. As designed.
- (a)-(d) unchanged. (c) slowest 25 ms (was 17 ms) and (e) slowest 427 ms (was 280 ms): NEW COST from RW1.1, see below.
- (e) 427 ms explained: hw-check calls SetPowerMode(Forced) then Read(). Read() now goes to sleep first (RW1.1); the chip is
  still in the forced measurement SetPowerMode started, so SetPowerMode(Sleep) is "leaving forced mode" and waits
  145 ms; then the stale read; then Read's own forced measurement (131 ms + polling). 145 + 145 + 131 ≈ 421 ms.
  Before RW1.1, Read() re-wrote forced (same mode, no wait) and returned the measurement SetPowerMode had started.
  Consequence: SetPowerMode(Forced) followed by Read() now takes two measurements. Read() alone is unaffected.
