## CLI reading (VERIFIED on this chip)
- After leaving normal mode mid-measurement, a new mode written before the running measurement ends is ignored,
  silently (no ERR bit). Waiting longer than the measurement fixes it.
- PWR_CTRL reads sleep immediately, so it can't be used to detect the window. Settings registers (OSR) are not
  affected. Not tested: the same for leaving forced mode mid-measurement (assumed alike; the fix covers it).
- Explains hw-check (b) of 003 and the 3 conf-diag mismatches of 004.
