## Desktop analysis (arithmetic, not a measurement)
Reference station pressure:
  29.89 inHg × 33.8639 hPa/inHg = 1012.19 hPa at sea level
  at 59.1 m: 1012.19 × exp(−59.1 / ~8400 m) ≈ 1012.19 − 7.1 ≈ 1005.1 hPa
Measured: 1005.5 ± 0.1 hPa → difference ≈ +0.4 hPa.
  BMP390 absolute accuracy (datasheet): ±0.5 hPa. Also uncertain: the house's elevation vs the city's 194 ft
  (4 m ≈ 0.5 hPa), the report's timing and rounding (0.01 inHg ≈ 0.34 hPa). → AGREES within the expected uncertainty.
Altitude using the reported sea-level pressure instead of 1013.25 hPa: 66 m − (1013.25 − 1012.19)/0.12 ≈ 57 m,
  vs 59 m city elevation. → consistent.
Lift test: at rest, run-to-run noise ≈ ±1 Pa (±0.01 hPa ≈ ±8 cm). During the hand movements the pressure moved by
  0.10-0.14 hPa (10-14 Pa) and the computed altitude by 0.8-1.2 m, i.e. the expected ~12 Pa/m for a 3-4 ft range.
  Movement was continuous and the IIR filter (coefficient 3) lags, so this is qualitative. A cleaner version: one
  full run held still on the floor, one held still ~1 m higher, compare the averages.
