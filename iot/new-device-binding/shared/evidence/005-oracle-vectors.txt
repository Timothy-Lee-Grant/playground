  V9: Bosch clamps T to -40 and reports P = 0 (pressure not computed after the temperature warning).
      Our U4 design returns null/false instead; oracle 2's unclamped values are recorded for the range tests.
  V10: Bosch clamps P to 125000; unclamped value ~125997 Pa (> 125000 -> our U4 gives null/false).
  V15: raw 0x800000/0x800000 (datasheet reset value) decodes to 23.68 °C / 79899 Pa with set A:
       the temperature alone is a normal room value (U5 collision risk); the PAIR needs ~23.7 °C at ~799 hPa.
  Agreement on in-range vectors is far inside the Step 4 stop threshold (0.01 °C / 1 Pa).
