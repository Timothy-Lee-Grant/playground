## 4. Discoveries
- **Datasheet timing formulas differ by chip** (verified, `001`): BMP390 §3.9.2 `234 + (392 + 2^osr_p·2020) + (163 + 2^osr_t·2020)` µs
  (revised in DS rev 1.1 "Changed the T_conv equation"); the BMP388 datasheet and Bosch's driver use `…2000…313…`. Brief §3 had the driver's constants. Implemented per chip.
- **Data registers reset to 0x800000** (verified, `001` table 25); raw temperature 0x800000 = 23.68 °C with calibration set A (verified, `005`) → U5 check requires **both** fields at the reset value.
- **I²C multi-byte writes are (register, value) pairs, not auto-increment** (verified, `001`, DS §5.2.1); the Step 8 card said otherwise.
- **Forced mode returns to sleep by itself** (verified, `001`, DS §3.3.2). **Soft-reset completion = EVENT 0x10 `por_detected`** (verified, `001`, DS §3.2); no soft-reset duration is given.
- **Legal mode transitions** (Figure 4 is vector art; unreadable here): **unverified**. The binding goes through Sleep between Forced and Normal.
- **`cmd_rdy` reads 1 when idle**: **unverified** (table 25 says STATUS resets to 0x00); the reset routine depends on it.
- **conf_err timing** (read right after writing Normal): **unverified**.
- ×1/×1 at 200 Hz: typical 4.82 ms < 5 ms period, max 5.70 ms > 5 ms (verified, `001` table 23); effect on a real chip in Normal mode **unverified**.
- **Bosch's out-of-range behavior** (verified, `005`): too cold → T clamped to −40 °C **and P = 0**; too high P → clamped to 125000 Pa.
- **O4 confirmed** (verified, `005`): Bosch's float path; C# double matches the datasheet formulas to the printed precision and Bosch to 8e-5 Pa (`008`).
- Repo facts (verified, `001`, `006`, `012`): CS1591/TreatWarningsAsErrors/SA1412 (BOM) enforced as **errors**; unused `private const` gives no warning; SDK 10 `dotnet sln add` auto-adds referenced projects and defaults to `.slnx`; `tools/device-listing` already knows barometer/altimeter/thermometer.
- Calibration-set provenance (sets A/B from a search summary of Bosch forum posts now 404): **unverified**; only used as realistic inputs.
