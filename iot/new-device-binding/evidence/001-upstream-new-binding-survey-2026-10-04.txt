## F. Bosch reference driver used to confirm BMP3xx register facts

github.com/boschsensortec/BMP3_SensorAPI @ db4cf8e (2022-07-12), BSD-3-Clause, bmp3_defs.h / bmp3.c:
  I2C address 0x76 (SDO low) / 0x77 (SDO high); CHIP_ID reg 0x00 = 0x50 (BMP388), 0x60 (BMP390)
  ERR 0x02, STATUS 0x03 (cmd_rdy 0x10, drdy_press 0x20, drdy_temp 0x40), DATA 0x04..0x09,
  INT_CTRL 0x19, IF_CONF 0x1A, PWR_CTRL 0x1B (mode sleep 0 / forced 1 / normal 3), OSR 0x1C, ODR 0x1D,
  CONFIG (IIR) 0x1F, calibration 21 bytes from 0x31, CMD 0x7E (soft reset 0xB6).
  Calibration: 14 coefficients, mixed u16/i16/i8, each scaled by a power of two (par_p1 and par_p2 are
  offset by 16384 first). Both a floating-point and an integer (int64) compensation path exist.
