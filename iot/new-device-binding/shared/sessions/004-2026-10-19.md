## 4. Discoveries
- **Verified (033, (f)):** datasheet table 29 holds on our BMP390. After an unread forced measurement STATUS = 0x70; one
  burst read of DATA → 0x10. Stale data-ready flags are real (R3).
- **Verified (033, (g0)):** R2 happens on real hardware. Raw register writes with the pre-fix 6 ms wait lost 3/30
  forced writes after x1 → x32 → x1 in normal mode at 160 ms. **Unverified:** why it's rarer than the worst-case
  estimate (~1/3 of tries); maybe the chip doesn't always finish an old-settings conversion.
- **Verified (033, (g)):** with the fix, 45/45, slowest 85 ms (≈ x32-pressure wait 76 ms + x1/x1 read).
- **Verified (033, (c)/(e)) and explained:** RW1.1 makes `SetPowerMode(Forced)` + `Read()` take two measurements. The
  slowest (e) went 280 → 427 ms (145 ms wait leaving forced + 145 + 131), (c) 17 → 25 ms. `Read()` alone costs +1
  write and a +6-byte read (~0.2 ms; **unverified** on hardware). Timothy chose to document it (D11).
- **Verified (026/028):** both red runs failed for the predicted reason, so the simulator now encodes R2 and R3.
- **Correction of my own 2026-10-17 entry** (plan line ~1060): "the max() … covers settings changed while in normal
  mode" was wrong for raise-then-lower. Corrected in the RW1.2 Stage 5 entry (entries are append-only).
- **Verified (032):** the R1 forced-read path never had the sentinel check, so `Read_MeasurementAtResetValue_ReturnsIt`
  passed before the fix. Only TryRead and normal-mode Read were affected.
- **Unverified:** which oversampling a running normal-mode conversion uses after an OSR change (fake and fix assume
  the slowest since mode entry).
